Problem - 

 

ABC.ai is a product that helps clients improve their brand presence in gen-AI platforms like ChatGPT, claude, gemini. Search is moving from google to AI platforms and the way people search is also changing .For brands, capturing customer intent and being present in the AI platforms when customers query for intent that match their brand is critical . 

 

ABC.ai wants to - 

Understand customer intent for a vertical / brand and a taxonomy per vertical that provides deeper understanding of the vertical
Generate realistic prompts around the intent 
Measure brand presence across intent types 
Suggest actions to improve brand presence
 

 

Based on this information 

 

How will you approach this problem for a vertical so that you provide base understanding of the vertical to the customer - say Beauty and cosmetics 
How will you tailor it for client - say Loreal 
How will you measure and suggest fixes across platform
How will you design the schema in a way that scales with addition of new vertical and client. 
How will you validate the accuracy of the results . 
 

Expected results 

 

working prototype to show your solution - Use of AI (like claude/ Codex) is encouraged 
Clear explanation of gaps in the prototype and challenges you will face in production and asks from you the solve the problem holistically.
Technical architecture diagram of the engine (Assume cloud / DB stack you are most familiar with . We want to understand logical architecture more than the exact cloud resources you will use)
